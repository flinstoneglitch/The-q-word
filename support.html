How to play

You have 6 tries to guess the day's 5-letter word.

  • 🟩 = right letter, right spot
  • 🟨 = right letter, wrong spot
  • ⬛ = letter not in the word

A new word is chosen every day using a live "hybrid" entropy source — local device entropy mixed with ANU QRNG, Amazon Braket, and IBM Quantum hardware when available. You can see today's source and entropy score in-game.

Common questions

Why does the background image change every day?
It's selected using that same daily random seed, pulled from Bing's daily image.

I found a bug, or the word won't update.
Email colin.damage@gmail.com with what happened and roughly when — a screenshot helps.

Is there an account or sign-in?
No — The Q Word has no accounts and doesn't collect any personal data. See the Privacy Policy.

⚛️ How today's word is chosen

Every day, a script runs a small experiment on a real IBM quantum computer: 8 qubits are placed in superposition — genuinely both 0 and 1 at once — then measured.

The measurement collapses them into the 8 digits shown in the footer. That bitstring becomes today's seed, which selects the secret word. No human, and no ordinary computer, decides it — quantum mechanics does.

The badge shows which IBM machine ran today's experiment, and each seed is verifiable via its IBM job ID.
